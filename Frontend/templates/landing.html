The Aligned Workflow

4 Phases. 10 Continuous Steps.

Every step seamlessly passes its output to the next, from role creation to final hiring decisions.

Phase 1 • Post

Post & Distribute

Generate tailored role specs, approve AI-drafted job postings, and publish instantly across top Pakistani boards.

1Job input: HR enters role title, skills, experience, PKR salary & hire count.
2AI JD generation: LLM drafts the description; HR reviews and approves.
3Multi-board posting: Live publishing to Rozee.pk and Indeed Pakistan.
Phase 2 • Screen

Screen & Shortlist

Collect inbound applications, run AI semantic CV matching, and automatically filter candidates.

4Application collection: Resumes arrive via dedicated inbox or the careers page.
5AI CV screening: Parsed into structured data and scored against criteria.
6Shortlist & notify: Top candidates shortlisted; rejections sent automatically.
Phase 3 • Interview

AI Video Interview

Autonomous 3-stage interviews with speech transcription and multi-parameter scorecards.

7Schedule & consent: Self-serve time picker with mandatory recording consent.
83-Stage AI interview: Screening → Technical → Behavioral.
9Speech analysis: Whisper transcription & 5-dimension scoring (0–10).
Phase 4 • Decide

Decide & Retain

Weighted composite leaderboard with explainable AI reasons and smart storage retention.

10Final ranking: Combined CV + interview score shown on the HR dashboard.
•AI rationale: A clear hiring justification is generated for each applicant.
•Retention policy: Recordings kept only for top candidates; the rest are purged.
